How to Use QenBel Smart Formatter

Complete walkthrough for preparing ID cards, Aadhaar long forms, and documents for high-resolution, print-ready Word and PDF exports.

1

Step 1: Add Card Photographs

Start by dragging and dropping photos of your document into the Front and Back upload slots, or click Browse to select files from your computer.

  • Supports .JPG .PNG .WEBP .BMP formats.
  • If processing a single-sided document, you can leave the back slot empty and continue.
  • Click Continue → to proceed to arrangement.
2

Step 2: Arrange Sides & Select Output Format

Confirm that the front and back sides are assigned correctly:

  • Click ⇄ Swap if the front and back photos were loaded in reverse order.
  • Select Output Format:
    • Card (86 × 54 mm): Standard ID-1 card dimensions for PAN cards, Voter IDs, Driving Licenses, PVC Aadhaar cards, and bank cards.
    • Long Form (210 × 85 mm): Specialized profile for Aadhaar letter bottom cut-slips.
  • Click Process Card → to run automated edge detection and perspective warping.
💡 Format Selection Tip
Selecting the correct format in Step 2 activates the specialized detection algorithm tailored to that card's physical aspect ratio.
3

Step 3: Automated Processing

The software executes an ultra-fast computer vision pipeline:

  • Foreground Isolation: Automatically segments hands, fingers, and background textures.
  • Corner Quad Extraction: Finds the physical card edges using Hough line and component analysis.
  • Perspective Rectification: Flattens angle distortion and skew into an upright, rectilinear document.
  • Enhancement: Optimizes contrast, cleans up lighting unevenness, and sharpens micro-text.
4

Step 4: Inspect Preview & Fine-Tune

Inspect the processed result with powerful operator controls:

  • Single-Click Front/Back Toggle: Click the top-right 👁 Preview: FRONT ⇄ button (or click directly anywhere on the image) to seamlessly flip between Front and Back views.
  • Rotation: Click ↶ Rotate Left or ↷ Rotate Right (90° increments). The system preserves exact native aspect ratios without squashing or quality degradation.
  • Edit Corners: Click Edit Front Corners or Edit Back Corners to drag the 4 corner handles manually if the card had damaged edges or extreme glare.
  • Zoom & Pan: Use the zoom controls (＋, －, Fit) to inspect text clarity and barcode readability.
5

Step 5: Export to Print-Ready Microsoft Word (.docx)

Configure your output print sheet and export directly:

  • Card Sizing & Layout Controls:
    • Placement Layout: Choose between Auto (Recommended), Stacked (Top / Bottom), or Side-by-Side.
    • Card Width (cm): Pre-populated with authentic physical dimensions (8.6 cm for Standard Card, 8.5 cm for vertical Aadhaar slip, 18.5 cm for horizontal slip). Fully adjustable to match your print shop's requirements.
    • Lock Aspect Ratio: Checked by default to guarantee zero stretching or distortion.
    • ↺ Reset: Restores exact 1:1 real-world physical dimensions anytime.
  • Clean Output Guarantee: The exported Word document contains ZERO extra text, title labels, or header stamps. Only the clean, high-resolution card images positioned exactly for instant printing.
  • Click 📄 EXPORT AS WORD (.DOCX), choose your save path, and open in Word to print!
🖨 Aadhaar Long Form Printing Tip
For vertical Aadhaar slips (UIDAI letter format), Side-by-Side layout places both Front and Back at genuine 1:1 physical size (8.5 cm × 21.0 cm each) on a standard A4 portrait page with zero squashing.
